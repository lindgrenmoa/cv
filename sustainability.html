ProfileProfil

I have worked on employment, labour rights and social protection since 2019, most recently at the International Labour Organization (ILO) in Geneva. My work there covered knowledge management, communications and monitoring for programmes in crisis- and disaster-affected settings, where climate change and just transition were recurring themes. I have met workers along a value chain to collect information on working conditions, coordinated international projects funded by Sida and the EU, and served as an elected health and safety representative. I am now completing an ESRS certification so that I can bring this experience to corporate sustainability reporting, especially the social standards on own workforce, workers in the value chain and affected communities. Colleagues have appreciated that I see what needs to be done without waiting to be asked, and that I am collaborative and considerate.

Je travaille sur l’emploi, les droits du travail et la protection sociale depuis 2019, dernièrement à l’Organisation internationale du Travail (OIT) à Genève. Je termine actuellement une certification ESRS afin de mettre cette expérience au service du reporting de durabilité des entreprises, en particulier des normes sociales sur les effectifs de l’entreprise, les travailleurs de la chaîne de valeur et les communautés concernées. Mes collègues ont apprécié que je voie ce qu’il y a à faire sans attendre qu’on me le demande, et que je sois collaborative et attentionnée.
